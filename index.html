About

Aerospace Engineer focused on Flight Dynamics and State Estimation for space applications. My work centers on orbital mechanics, high-fidelity orbit propagation, orbit determination, and nonlinear filtering for spacecraft state estimation.

I develop technical projects combining GMAT simulations and Python-based numerical modeling, with emphasis on perturbation analysis (J2, atmospheric drag, SRP), covariance assessment, residual analysis and Extended Kalman Filtering.

My objective is to contribute to operational Flight Dynamics and GNC activities, supporting orbit maintenance, estimation performance, maneuver design, and on-orbit mission analysis.

Technical Projects

High-Fidelity Orbit Propagation and Perturbation Analysis

Orbital Mechanics · GMAT · Numerical Propagation · Sensitivity Analysis

Development of a high-fidelity orbital propagation framework to assess the impact of perturbations on LEO spacecraft trajectories.

Includes comparison between 2-body and perturbed models (J2, drag, SRP), long-term trajectory divergence assessment, and sensitivity analysis with respect to ballistic coefficient variations.

Batch Orbit Determination and Covariance Analysis

Least Squares · State Estimation · Residual Analysis · Observability

Implementation of a Batch Least Squares orbit determination algorithm using simulated range and range-rate measurements with realistic noise.

Includes residual evaluation, convergence assessment, covariance matrix computation, and analysis of estimation sensitivity to tracking geometry.

Extended Kalman Filter for Nonlinear Orbit Estimation

Nonlinear Dynamics · EKF · Covariance Propagation · GNC

Design and implementation of an Extended Kalman Filter for recursive nonlinear orbit estimation.

Pose Estimation · OBB-TM Algorithm · Performance Analysis · Orbital Robotics
