About

Aerospace Engineer focused on Flight Dynamics, Mission Analysis, and State Estimation for space applications. My work centers on orbital mechanics, constellation design, interplanetary trajectory optimization, and nonlinear filtering for spacecraft navigation.

I develop technical projects combining GMAT simulations and Python-based numerical modeling, with an emphasis on perturbation analysis, Walker constellation coverage optimization, Lambert's problem solvers, and recursive state estimation (EKF).

My objective is to contribute to operational Flight Dynamics, Mission Analysis, and GNC activities, supporting trajectory design, constellation management, and advanced orbit determination workflows.

Technical Projects

High-Fidelity Orbit Propagation and Perturbation Analysis

Orbital Mechanics · GMAT · Numerical Propagation · Sensitivity Analysis

Development of a high-fidelity orbital propagation framework to assess the impact of perturbations on LEO spacecraft trajectories.

Includes comparison between 2-body and perturbed models (J2, drag, SRP), long-term trajectory divergence assessment, and sensitivity analysis with respect to ballistic coefficient variations.

Trajectory Optimization · Lambert's Problem · Astropy & Poliastro · Python

Numerical framework for interplanetary mission analysis, featuring automated Porkchop plot generation, Lambert's problem solver, and 3D heliocentric trajectory visualization.

LEO Walker Delta Constellation Optimization

Constellation Design · Coverage Analysis · Revisit Time · Visibility Geometry

Design and optimization framework for a Walker Delta satellite constellation in LEO to ensure continuous coverage over a specific regional Area of Interest (e.g., Mediterranean basin).

Analyzes revisit times, maximum gaps, and elevation angles to determine the optimal trade-off between the number of orbital planes, satellites per plane, and coverage performance.

Batch Orbit Determination and Covariance Analysis

Least Squares · State Estimation · Residual Analysis · Observability

Implementation of a Batch Least Squares orbit determination algorithm using simulated range and range-rate measurements with realistic noise.

Includes residual evaluation, convergence assessment, covariance matrix computation, and analysis of estimation sensitivity to tracking geometry.
